As for the website itself, I wanted to highlight its importance, precisely because I made it with the idea of helping people learn more about the oceans, understand their importance and their biodiversity—which is very rich—and just how important it is to the world. That is what the website is about. Building it was simple; it was a quick way to make it, an idea that I had and wanted to bring to life.
Just one detail: when it came to translating the words into English, in the Readme and other things, I ended up using Google Translate. Because of that, what I am saying might turn out very literal, which could change the meaning of the words, making it look a bit strange or sounding like I used AI.
